# Asset and Facilities Management Template Pack

This template pack covers the kinds of forms that asset managers and facilities managers use regularly across housing, estates, and building operations.

It focuses on forms that are still commonly handled through PDFs, spreadsheets, email chains, paper checklists, or disconnected contractor systems rather than a dedicated mobile/web workflow.

It is intended to help with:
- stock condition and planned maintenance,
- statutory compliance,
- communal area and estate inspections,
- external area and plant checks,
- and follow-up action tracking.

---

## 1. Common workflow

A practical asset/facilities workflow often looks like this:

1. An inspection or compliance cycle is scheduled.
2. A site visit or desktop review is carried out.
3. Condition, defects, compliance items, and priorities are recorded.
4. Photos, meter readings, or evidence are attached.
5. Remedial works or follow-up tasks are raised.
6. Sign-off or review is completed.
7. The data is filed into an asset register, works programme, or compliance log.

## 14. Why these forms are good automation targets

These forms are repetitive, structured, and evidence-heavy.

They usually need:
- location context,
- photos,
- condition scoring,
- priority ratings,
- sign-off,
- and follow-up task creation.

That makes them ideal for:
- mobile capture on site,
- web review by managers,
- automatic PDF report generation,
- and export into asset or maintenance systems.

---

## 15. Product opportunity note

A focused mobile/web workflow for asset and facilities teams could cover:

- selecting a site or asset,
- walking through an inspection checklist,
- capturing photos and readings,
- assigning defects to a priority list,
- and producing a branded report.

That would be especially useful for councils, housing associations, FM contractors, and property teams that still rely on manual PDFs and spreadsheets.

## 16. Next implementation step

If these forms are to become product features, the next practical step is to define:
- the asset model,
- the inspection model,
- the defect/action model,
- the compliance checklist structure,
- and the report output schema.

That would allow a single mobile capture flow to support multiple inspection types without rebuilding the workflow every time.
